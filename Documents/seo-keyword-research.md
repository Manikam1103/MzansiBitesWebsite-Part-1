# SEO Keyword Research: Mzansi Bites Bakery

**Method.** Search volumes were not taken from a paid tool (e.g. Google Keyword Planner needs an ad account), so volume is not quoted. Keywords were chosen by (1) the products and services in `menu.json`, (2) the local intent a Durban customer has ("near me", suburb/city), (3) wording seen on Durban bakery and cake listings (Culture Trip, 2026; Eat Out, 2026) and (4) Google autocomplete-style long-tail variations. Intent: **T** = transactional, **I** = informational, **L** = local.

| Page | Primary keyword | Secondary / long-tail keywords | Intent |
|------|-----------------|--------------------------------|--------|
| index.html | bakery Durban | fresh bread Durban, South African bakery, family bakery Durban | L / T |
| menu.html | bakery menu Durban | koeksisters price, vetkoek, sourdough Durban, milk tart slice | T |
| enquiry.html | custom cake Durban | cake quote Durban, order cake online Durban, bakery catering Durban | T |
| about.html | family bakery KwaZulu-Natal | about Mzansi Bites, Durban bakery team | I |
| gallery.html | celebration cake designs | bakery photos Durban, birthday cake ideas | I |
| contact.html | bakery near me Durban | bakery opening hours Durban, directions to bakery | L |

## How keywords are used
- Primary keyword appears in the `<title>`, meta description, the `<h1>` or first `<h2>`, and the first paragraph of its page.
- Titles are kept to about 60 characters and meta descriptions under 160 characters.
- Keywords are written naturally (no stuffing); `meta keywords` is included only for completeness because search engines ignore it.
- Image file names carry keywords (e.g. `butter-croissants-pastries-600.jpg`), plus descriptive alt text.
