# Mzansi Bites Bakery — Content Research and Sourcing

This document accompanies the Website Project Proposal and HTML foundation
for Mzansi Bites Bakery. It records the content gathered for the site and
where visual assets should be sourced from before final submission.

## 1. Organisation Content (Researched/Drafted)
Since Mzansi Bites Bakery is a fictional client, the following content was
drafted to be realistic for a small South African bakery business, based on
common industry practices and typical product/pricing research for
Durban-based bakeries:

- **History:** Family-run bakery, founded 2019, Durban, KwaZulu-Natal
- **Mission/Vision statements:** drafted in the Website Project Proposal
- **Product range & indicative pricing:** based on typical South African
  bakery pricing (breads R25–R45, pastries R18–R35, cakes R280–R550+)
- **Trading hours:** typical small-bakery hours (Mon–Fri 07:00–18:00,
  Sat 08:00–15:00, closed Sunday)
